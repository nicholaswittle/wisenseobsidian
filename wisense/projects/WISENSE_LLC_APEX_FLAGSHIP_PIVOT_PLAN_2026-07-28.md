## 2. Selling Points & Core Operational Modules

### 🍕 Module 1: Commission-Free Guest Online Ordering ($99/mo OS Value)
* **The Problem:** Third-party ordering apps charge 15% to 30% per order, eating up restaurant margins.
* **The Apex Solution:** A branded guest ordering storefront link (`/app?token=yourvenue`) with **0% commission**. Direct Stripe Connect payout into the owner's bank account with a flat 1.5% WiSense payment rail fee.
* **Annual Savings:** A venue doing $10,000/month in online orders saves **$2,850/month ($34,200/year)** compared to 30% DoorDash fees.

### ⏱️ Module 2: 3-Tap Staff Scheduling & Labor Guardrails (Included)
* **The Problem:** Paper schedules, frantic text messages for shift swaps, and surprise overtime fines.
* **The Apex Solution:** 3-tap mobile schedule publishing, instant shift swap marketplace with manager approval, AI-assisted call-out priority routing, and automatic PA labor law overtime warnings.

### 🖨️ Module 3: Kitchen Console (KDS) & Thermal Printing (Included)
* **The Problem:** Expensive dedicated KDS hardware and complex POS integrations.
* **The Apex Solution:** Runs on any iPad, tablet, or browser. Includes audio chimes, 90-second ticket waiting escalations, prep minute overrides, and direct Star TSP100 80mm thermal receipt printing.

### 📱 Module 4: Automated Twilio SMS Fan-Out (Included)
* **The Problem:** Customers missing pickup notifications or kitchen staff unaware of rush spikes.
* **The Apex Solution:** Automatic SMS texts sent to customers when orders are ready for pickup, plus SMS alerts sent to kitchen managers during order escalations.

### 🛡️ Module 5: Auto-Pause Capacity Engine (Included)
* **The Problem:** Kitchen getting slammed during Friday night rushes, leading to bad reviews and cold food.
* **The Apex Solution:** Realtime order throttling that automatically pauses online ordering or extends prep estimates when hourly order volume hits capacity thresholds.
