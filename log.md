- **[JIGSY NUMBERS REFRAME THE REVENUE MODEL]**: Pulled their Square reporting. Net sales **$291,668** (-5% YoY), tips $39,813 (13.6% of net), refunds $136, average sale $48 (**+11%**) -- but **orders -14% and customers -12%**. Traffic is the problem, not spend; ticket size is masking the decline. **That is the pitch: recover order count, not "modernise your website."** WARNING: **Labor % of net sales is 0.0% -- they track no labor in Square at all**: no scheduling, no timeclock, no labor cost, on a $292k base. Apex has all three and Square will not fill it. **Ordering fees are not a business**: 1.5% at a realistic 10-25% online capture is **$36-91/month** from Jigsy. One venue on a $79/mo subscription is $948/yr -- more than double, with no volume risk. **Reframe: ordering is the wedge, the scheduler is the business.** Full note: [[wisense/projects/JIGSYS_BUSINESS_NUMBERS_AND_REVENUE_MODEL_2026-08-01]].
- **[SMALL STRIPE VENUES MAY BE NET-NEGATIVE]**: On Stripe's "platform sets pricing" Connect model the platform pays $2/mo per active account + 0.25% + 25c per payout + 0.25% of payout volume. Net is about **1.0% of volume - $2 - payout fees**, so with daily payouts roughly `0.01xV - $9.50`/month and **break-even near $950/month of online volume** -- below that a Stripe venue *costs* money every month. **Square carries no per-account Connect charges**, so it is materially better economics for small venues, which is most of the target market. Second independent reason to favour the Square rail beyond free printing, and it makes `scripts/check_connect_pricing.mjs` urgent rather than optional.
- **[JIGSY TIP CONFIG CONFIRMED]**: Collect Tips ON, Smart Tip Amounts, calculated **after** taxes, custom amounts allowed, ask-before-payment OFF -- all inherited automatically by our hosted checkout via `allow_tipping`, no configuration needed on our side. Their in-person tipping runs on **paper receipts**, where Square settles with no tip if not entered within 36 hours; online tips bypass that window entirely, a small genuine improvement to mention. Also noted: **two duplicate "Appetizers" categories** in their Square Library -- harmless now, would duplicate if menu sync from Square is ever built.
