THE RULE

Each phase closes against a written gate — a goal you can measure, not a feeling. No new thing ships until the gate before it is met. This is the Sun Strategy: the core earns before any satellite is built.

THE TRAP TO AVOID

Building satellites as a "platform" with no customers. The failure mode is 10 apps, zero revenue. One strong product → one satellite → then the family. Each step proves the next.

THE FIRST SATELLITE

Pick Supply (inventory) or Pulse (reputation/loyalty) — closest to the transaction without being regulated. Avoid Capital & Guard early (lending/insurance need licenses + compliance).

North star: $300/mo, three venues. The ecosystem is the eventual shape, not today's goal. Keep the vision; let the gates drive the sequence. The v3 operations layer (17 registered ops, domain_events, actor_kind) is already 80% of the API — Phase 3 is mostly packaging, not building.
