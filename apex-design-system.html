The rule: everything is neutral charcoal (#141517 → #2e3134). Coral (#ff7f50) is the accent — buttons, the AI orb, the "needs you" left-border, warnings. Green (#6fcf97) means healthy. There are no warm-tinted dark surfaces — every background is charcoal, never brown/orange-tinted. This is what Nick wants and what keeps Claude from "making everything orange."
