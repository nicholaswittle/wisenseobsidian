The rule, made concrete: to make and edit a schedule, an owner gets exactly 3 buttons — Add a shift, Edit a shift, Publish/Pause this week. Not a toolbar, not a menu, not a list of twelve actions. The draft model does the heavy lifting: shifts pile up invisibly, and the single "Publish this week" button is the one place everyone gets told. When there's a draft, the button says Publish (coral); when it's live, it becomes Pause. Same three buttons, every state, nothing to hunt for.
