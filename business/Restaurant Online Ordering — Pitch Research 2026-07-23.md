## The core argument — margin math (owned vs. marketplace)

The money slide. Worked on a realistic ~$45 pizza ticket, 30% food cost:

| | Marketplace (25%) | **Owned site (~3% card)** |
|---|---:|---:|
| Subtotal | $45.00 | $45.00 |
| Food cost (30%) | −$13.50 | −$13.50 |
| Platform fee | −$11.25 | −$1.35 |
| **Gross profit kept** | **$20.25** | **$30.15** |

→ **~$10 more per order (~50% more margin) on the owned channel.** At 20
orders/day ≈ ~$70K/yr the marketplace would skim. **The avoided commission is
the ROI that funds the build** — the reusable close for any restaurant pitch.

## Reusable cited stats (for future pitches)

- Online orders **18–40% larger** than phone; pizza chains saw **+18% spend**
  online vs phone. Online tickets avg **$32.24 vs $18.99** phone (Thrive POS).
- Online customers order **~67% more frequently**; **~70% prefer ordering
  direct** over marketplaces.
- **~75%** of restaurant traffic off-premise; digital ≈ **⅓ of sales** (was 12%
  pre-pandemic). **84%** of pizzerias sell online, avg **26.9% of sales**.
- Marketplace commissions **15–30%** (often **30–40% all-in**) vs owned = flat
  fee / ~3% card. Pizzeria food cost **28–32%**, gross margin **65–75%**.
- Case studies (vendor-published, directional): Prima $5.1M, Vinny's $3.1M,
  Taste of NY $2.1M, City View **+32% online in 3 months**.

## Competitive finding (Enola / West Shore)

Jigsy's peers **all have online ordering** — The Pizza Grille (own Toast site +
DoorDash/Grubhub), Pizzatown USA (own site/Slice + marketplaces), Nikoli's (own
site). **Jigsy's is the only notable peer with none.** The leaders lead with
their *own* links (margin) and use marketplaces as secondary reach — the exact
model to sell.
