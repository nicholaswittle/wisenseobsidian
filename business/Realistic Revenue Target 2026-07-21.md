## The Goal

$300/month. That's it. Not $10,000. Not an agency. One client paying $300.

## Why This Is Realistic

- One website client at $300 = goal hit
- One Fiverr resume order at $50-75 = 4-6 orders/month = goal hit
- One website at $800 one-time = 2.5 months of costs covered
- One retainer at $300/mo = goal hit month 1 and every month after

## The Simplest Path

Pick ONE: websites (best shot — live portfolio exists).

Target: ONE client. Not 10. Not 20. One person paying $300.

### Acquisition (all remote, zero cost)

1. Fiverr gig already live — wait for orders
2. Cold email 5 businesses with bad websites — send live demo link
3. Jigsy's social media post — when owner posts, first inquiry comes in
4. Post in 1-2 local business Facebook groups

### Pricing for the first client

- Option A: $300 one-time for a 3-page site (covers 1 month)
- Option B: $300/month retainer — website + hosting + maintenance + review responses (covers every month, recurring)
- Option C: $800 one-time for full site (covers 2.5 months)

Recommend Option B once you have the first client. One-time gets you the first $300. Retainer keeps it coming.

## What Not to Do

- Don't build a complex agency
- Don't buy GoHighLevel ($97-297/mo)
- Don't run Facebook ads
- Don't cold call
- Don't try to get 10 clients at once
- Don't overthink pricing tiers
