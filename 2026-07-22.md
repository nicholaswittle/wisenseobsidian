## Advice / one-offs (reference)

- **Fiverr scam flagged:** an instant message on a brand-new gig
  (`bengarcia8262`, urgency + a Google-Doc link) = classic new-seller phishing.
  Don't click, **report + block**, keep everything on-platform.
- **W-9:** fill it out as whoever actually receives payment. A single-member
  LLC with no corporate election is a **disregarded entity** — line 1 = your
  name, line 2 = LLC, check "Individual/sole proprietor or single-member LLC",
  TIN = your SSN (or the LLC's EIN). Wrong name/TIN combo is what makes
  Fiverr's TIN check bounce; confirm any S-corp election with the accountant.
- **AI consultant @ PA State Police:** you don't get "hired" — you become a
  registered Commonwealth vendor and win via procurement (PA Supplier Portal,
  SB/SDB cert, IT ITQ). AI is centralized at OA, and **CJIS clearance** is the
  real wall. Realistic entry = subcontract under a prime or a small pilot; PSP
  is a hard *first* client for a pre-revenue LLC. Sequence easier references
  first.
- **Resume sample:** built a fictional Marine→civilian resume + 1-page PDF for
  the Fiverr gig demo, plus a reusable Gemini prompt for variants.
