# ☕ Denim Coffee Harrisburg — Pitch Audit & Demo Strategy

> **Target:** Denim Coffee Company (Harrisburg Shops: 401 Walnut St & 17 S 2nd St)  
> **Location:** Harrisburg, PA · (717) 961-5555  
> **Current Page:** `https://www.denimcoffeecompany.com/pages/denim-coffee-harrisburg-pa` (Shopify Store Template)  
> **Live Redesign Demo:** [https://denimcoffeesite.vercel.app](https://denimcoffeesite.vercel.app)

---

## 🚨 Audited Weaknesses on Their Current Site

1. **E-Commerce / Retail Mismatch:** The current page is built on a Shopify retail shipping template meant for sending 12oz bean bags across the US, **NOT for local mobile coffee pickup in Downtown Harrisburg**.
2. **Hard-to-Find Cafe Menu:** Commuters rushing to work down Walnut St or 2nd St cannot quickly view lattes, iced drinks, pastries, or acai bowls on a mobile phone without clicking through multiple nested store links.
3. **No 1-Tap Mobile Pickup Cart:** Forces local coffee drinkers to wait in long morning counter lines instead of ordering mobile pickup ahead of time.
4. **No Real-Time Hours Status Pill:** Lacks a dynamic indicator letting morning commuters know if the cafe is open right now.

---

## 🚀 The WiSense Redesign Demo Features ([denimcoffeesite.vercel.app](https://denimcoffeesite.vercel.app))

1. **WiSense Standard Glassmorphism Topbar:** Features dynamic real-time status pill (`🟢 Open Now til 3 PM`), theme switcher (Dark / Light saved in `localStorage`), and floating mobile cart.
2. **Interactive Cafe Menu Board:** Tabbed categories (`Espresso & Lattes`, `Flash Chilled Brews`, `Acai & Bakery`, `Roasted Bean Bags`) with live `+ Add to Cart` buttons.
3. **Slide-Over Mobile Pickup Cart (`#cart-drawer`):** Allows morning commuters to order mobile pickup for 401 Walnut St or 17 S 2nd St.
4. **Barista Counter Rush Controls (`/staff.html`):** Turnkey tablet portal for baristas featuring a 1-tap **"Rush Mode" (+10m delay)** button and **"Pause Mobile Orders"** toggle.
