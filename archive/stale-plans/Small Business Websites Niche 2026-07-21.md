## The Niche

Small businesses (restaurants, plumbers, salons, contractors, auto shops) that have:
- No website at all (just a Facebook page)
- A terrible outdated website from 2015
- A half-finished Wix/Squarespace they never completed

These businesses need a clean, fast, mobile-friendly website. They don't need a custom React app. They need 1-5 pages that make them look professional.

## What to Offer

1-5 page websites:
- Home page (who they are, what they do)
- Menu / Services page
- About page
- Contact page with form
- Optional: photo gallery
- Optional: online ordering / cart (like Jigsy's)

## Pricing — Recurring Model (Claude's proposal 2026-07-23)

**$299 setup + $79/month — single plan, no tiers**

- Setup: covers build labor without $1,499 sticker shock
- $79/mo: hosting + maintenance + content updates (menus, specials, hours, events)
- One plan = less decision friction for a beginner seller and buyer
- Buyout option: client leaves and wants the code → one-time fee, hand over repo. Clean exit.
- Introduce Essentials ($49) / Plus ($99) tiers later once there's a client base

**Ownership model:**
- Client keeps their domain (their name, SEO, email — the assets that matter to them)
- WiSense builds the site and hosts on WiSense's Vercel
- WiSense owns the code; monthly fee licenses use + hosting + upkeep
- This is what makes the recurring model defensible

**Why recurring beats one-time:**
- Lower barrier than $800-1,500 upfront
- Predictable MRR vs feast/famine
- Restaurant niche fits — menus change constantly, "we keep it updated" is real value
- Site lives on WiSense infra = low casual churn
- $79/mo × 3 years = $2,844 per client vs one $800 check

**Economics:**
- Vercel Pro ($20/mo) required — free Hobby plan is non-commercial per ToS
- Pro hosts many client sites/domains under one account, so hosting cost stays ~$20 flat
- 10 clients = ~$790/mo revenue on ~$20 cost = near-pure margin

**Jigsy's reference point:** Built as a gift/portfolio piece. Live at jigsyssite.vercel.app. NOT a paying client. Use as demo for cold pitches.

## How to Build Fast (2-4 hours per site)

1. Client fills out a Google Form: business name, what they do, services/menu items, hours, contact info, photos, branding/colors
2. Feed into Claude: "Build a clean 4-page website for [business type] with this content: [paste]. Use React + Tailwind. Home, services, about, contact with form."
3. Claude generates the code
4. Review, fix, customize
5. Deploy to Vercel or Netlify (free)
6. Point their domain to it

At $800 per site and 2-4 hours build time, that's $200-400/hour.

## Client Acquisition (100% Remote)

### Method 1: Free Sample Demo
1. Google Maps → search "restaurants near me" / "plumbers near me" / "salons near me"
2. Click businesses with no website link or a bad website
3. Build a demo version of their site using AI (1 hour)
4. Deploy to Vercel (free) — get a live URL
5. Email or DM: "I rebuilt your website. Here's the live demo: [link]. If you like it, it's $800. If you want changes, I'll adjust for free."
6. They can SEE the result before paying. Not pitching — showing.

### Method 2: Facebook Groups
- Post in local business Facebook groups: "I build websites for small businesses. $800 flat. 1-week turnaround. DM me."

### Method 3: Fiverr Gig
- "I will build a modern website for your restaurant or small business"
- Packages: $500 (3-page) / $800 (5-page) / $1,500 (e-commerce with cart)

### Method 4: Referrals
- Every happy client → ask for 1 intro to another business owner
- Offer $50 discount to both on a successful referral

## Hosting = Recurring Revenue

- Deploy on Vercel (free) or Netlify (free)
- Charge client $25-50/mo for "hosting and maintenance"
- Pure profit — hosting costs nothing
- 10 clients = $250-500/mo recurring for near-zero work
- Updates (new menu items, price changes, photos): $25-50 per update or include 2/month in the maintenance fee
