# Market Positioning — Where WiSense Sits

## The WiSense Position

**Privacy-first mobile tools for underserved niches.**

Not enterprise. Not consumer SaaS. Not ad-supported. WiSense builds focused apps that work on-device, respect privacy, and solve problems Nicholas has lived.

## Competitive Landscape

### COMMS LINK
| Competitor | Model | WiSense Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Calm, Headspace | Subscription, cloud, data-harvesting | No cloud, no account, no retention, one-time price |
| Veteran-specific apps (VA apps) | Clunky, government-built | Polished Flutter, actually usable, no login |
| Generic journaling apps | Broad, not crisis-aware | Built-in crisis detection, debrief FSM, Gemma on-device |

### Apex Scheduler
| Competitor | Model | WiSense Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| When I Work, Deputy | Enterprise, per-seat, complex | Mobile-first, simple, brewpub-priced |
| Homegrown spreadsheets | Free but manual | Automated, clock-in, labor cost tracking |
| Google Calendar | Free but not scheduling-specific | Shifts, swaps, time clock, sidework |

### New Horizon
| Competitor | Model | WiSense Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| TripIt, Kayak | Solo-first, ad-heavy | Couples-first, consensus alignment, no ads |
| Expedia/Viator direct | Booking only, no planning | AI-curated, affiliate links (same inventory, better UX) |
| Hopper | Price prediction only | Full trip planning + alignment, not just flights |

## Brand Pillars

1. **Privacy first** — no data leaves your device unless you choose (COMMS LINK, Apex local-first)
2. **Built by someone who gets it** — veteran, trooper, father — real user, not a tech bro
3. **Simple on purpose** — 3 focused apps, not a feature-bloated suite
4. **Respectful pricing** — pay once or subscribe fairly, no ads, no data selling

## Messaging

- **Tagline (company)**: "Tools that respect you."
- **COMMS LINK**: "Your debrief stays yours."
- **Apex**: "Scheduling that doesn't spy on your staff."
- **New Horizon**: "Plan trips together, not separately."

## Positioning Risks

- "Privacy-first" is crowded (Proton, Signal, etc.) — need to own the *mobile utility* niche, not the *privacy* niche
- No Mac = no iOS at launch = leaving premium users on table
- Affiliate revenue for New Horizon is thin margins — need volume
