# Pricing Strategy — WiSense Apps

How to price Flutter apps that respect users and sustain the business.

## Framework

| Model | Best For | WiSense Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time purchase | Utility apps, privacy-first | COMMS LINK | Low friction, no recurring cost, aligns with "no cloud" message |
| Subscription | SaaS with ongoing value | Apex Scheduler | Teams expect recurring billing; solo users want free |
| Affiliate/commission | Content/booking apps | New Horizon | No paywall — Viator/Expedia clicks fund the app |
| Freemium | Apps with clear free/paid divide | All three | Free tier = hook, paid tier = commitment |

## COMMS LINK

- **Recommended**: $7.99 one-time, free 7-day trial
- **Why**: No cloud cost to us = no recurring cost to justify. Privacy = premium.
- **Alternative**: Free with $2.99 "pro" unlock (export, voice journaling, custom themes)
- **Don't do**: Subscription — users will reject monthly for a local-only app

## Apex Scheduler

- **Recommended**: Free for 1 org (solo), $9/mo per additional org or $79/yr
- **Why**: Solo brewpub owner = free (goodwill, adoption). Multi-location = pays.
- **Pilot**: Free for Jigsy's until stable, then convert
- **Don't do**: Per-seat pricing — brewpubs have high staff turnover, feels punitive

## New Horizon

- **Recommended**: Free, affiliate-only
- **Why**: Couples won't pay for a travel planning app when competitors are free
- **Upside**: Viator tours (3–8% commission), Expedia hotels (2–5%)
- **Future**: Premium tier ($4.99/mo) for AI itinerary generation, budget tracking, group travel
- **Don't do**: Paywall the core alignment flow — it kills the viral loop

## Cross-App

- **Bundle**: If all 3 are on Play Store, offer bundle discount ($12.99 for all three, saves ~$5)
- **Family/Team plans**: Apex org pricing naturally covers this
- **No ads**: Ever. Privacy-first brand = no ad revenue. Period.
