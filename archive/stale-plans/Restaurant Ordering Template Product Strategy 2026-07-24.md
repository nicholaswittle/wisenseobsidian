# Restaurant Ordering Template — Product Strategy

> The Jigsy's build is NOT a one-off. It's the first configuration of a reusable restaurant ordering platform. Whether Jigsy's says yes or no, the template gets built and resold.

## The Strategy

Build the full ordering system as a **template** with restaurant-specific info as configuration, not hardcoded:

- Restaurant name, logo, colors, contact info
- Menu, modifiers, prices, taxes, availability
- Hours and pickup-time options
- Online ordering fee ($0.99 or $1.99) and tax treatment
- Manual payment or Square-connected payment mode
- Staff access, notification, printer, reporting settings

Jigsy's is the first configuration. If they approve, they're the first client. If they decline or delay, the template is already built — configure it for the next restaurant and sell.

## Why This Works

- No dependency on Jigsy's decision
- The demo is already live and working
- Square Sandbox checkout is verified
- 52-item menu, staff console, sold-out controls, daily reports — all reusable
- Each new restaurant = configuration, not a rebuild
- Build once, sell many times

## Pricing Per Restaurant

- Setup: $299 (configure menu, branding, hours, Square connection)
- Monthly: $79/mo (hosting, maintenance, content updates)
- OR: $0.99-$1.99/order fee model (risk-free for owner, passive income for WiSense)

## What's Already Built (Reusable)

- Customer ordering page with cart, modifiers, pickup scheduling
- Staff console: Accept & Print, Reject, pause/reopen, sold-out controls, prep time
- Daily reports with archived dates
- Square Sandbox checkout with delayed capture (verified)
- 80mm thermal printer ticket format
- "One website, optional ordering" — pause hides ordering buttons, site stays
- 52-item priced menu across 8 categories

## What's Still Needed (Template-Level)

- Hosted order database (shared across customer + staff devices)
- Password-protected staff access
- Live new-order notifications (push/SMS)
- Customer SMS for accepted/rejected confirmation
- Durable accepted-order and monthly-fee reporting
- Refund/cancellation workflow
- Backup procedure for internet or printer failure
- Production Square app credentials
- Multi-tenant: one deployment serves multiple restaurants

## Sales Pitch for Any Restaurant

"You're losing $10/order to DoorDash. I'll build you a direct ordering site — you keep that $10. $299 setup + $79/mo, or $0.99/order with zero upfront. If nobody orders, you pay nothing. If it works, we both win."

Show them the Jigsy's demo as proof it works. Configure their menu. Deploy. Repeat.
